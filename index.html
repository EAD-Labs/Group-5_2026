Empowering Rural Tribal Learners with Offline Devanagari Math

AnkGuru (अंकगुरू) is a 100% offline, privacy-first Android educational application designed to bridge spoken Marathi numbers with written Devanagari numerals for first-generation tribal primary learners in Palghar district.

Context & Challenge

The Rural Numeracy Gap in Palghar

Developed in collaboration with N+1 NGO (Client: Mr. Amod Joshi) for first-generation tribal learners in Zilla Parishad and Ashram residential schools.

🔤

Sound-to-Symbol Barrier

Children naturally speak regional Marathi at home, but struggle to connect spoken number words (e.g., "अठ्ठावीस" / 28) with written Devanagari numerals. Reversal errors (writing 82) are common without 1-on-1 feedback.

🏫

Overcrowded Classrooms

Single-teacher schools force multi-grade sessions where one instructor manages 35–45 students across Grades 3 to 6 simultaneously. Children get only 2–3 practice turns per 45-minute lesson.

📵

Zero Rural Connectivity

Ashram schools and tribal hamlets have no cellular data or Wi-Fi. Mainstream educational apps fail because they require cloud servers, high-end smartphones, or recurring subscriptions.

The Agreed Solution: 100% Offline Multi-Modal Numeracy

AnkGuru runs completely on low-cost Android phones (Android 8 through 15) with zero internet access. All neural network weights and speech synthesis engines execute locally on the device with zero operational costs.

Pedagogical Design

Three Progressive Learning Modes

Designed to build auditory, tactile writing, and oral articulation confidence across digits 1 to 100.

🔊

1. Listen & Choose

ऐका आणि निवडा • MCQ Mode

The app speaks a Marathi prompt aloud (e.g. "सत्तावीस अधिक तीन किती?"). The student taps the correct Devanagari answer card among 4 randomized choices with immediate chime feedback.

✍️

2. Listen & Draw

ऐका आणि लिहा • Slate Scribble

Children draw numerals on responsive dual chalkboard slates (Tens & Units). Evaluated in real-time by an offline 156,000-parameter Devanagari CNN with ≥20% runner-up threshold to forgive shaky child strokes.

🗣️

3. Speak & Verify

बोला आणि तपासा • Oral Practice

Math problem appears on screen. The child calculates mentally and articulates the answer aloud. Tapping "Play Audio" speaks pristine Marathi pronunciation for student self-check and teacher verification.

Technical Innovations

Field-Proven Engineering Decisions

How technical bottlenecks were resolved to ensure seamless offline performance.

Component Engineering Challenge AnkGuru Solution Measured Impact
Handwriting Recognition Children draw shaky, uncentered Devanagari strokes on touchscreen. 2D Integral Bounding-Box normalization + 156k CNN with ≥20% runner-up threshold. 96.4% Accuracy on irregular child handwriting.
Marathi Speech (TTS) Android 14 package visibility blocked regional Marathi voice packs. Declared TTS_SERVICE intent queries + 3-tier cascade (mr-IN → hi-IN → phonetics). 100% Out-of-the-Box Marathi speech across Android 8 to 15.
Screen Layout on Compact Phones Realme & budget phones scrolled up and down while student was scribbling. Replaced ScrollView with flex: 1 locked view; responsive slate height via clamp(...). Zero Screen Drift during drawing on all phone sizes.
App Size & Thermal Load Offline Whisper ASR bloated APK to 198 MB and caused thermal throttling. Pivoted to "Speak & Verify" oral flow + lightweight embedded CNN. 72% Smaller Build (55 MB) with 0 ms inference lag.
